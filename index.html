Printing

  1. Print at Actual size / 100%, never "Fit to page."
  2. Set paper type to Glossy photo paper.
  3. Let it dry 10 to 15 minutes before touching.
  4. Cut on the marks, then round corners at 3 mm.

Hybrid or other symbols

The buttons fill this in for you. For hybrid, type {W/U}. For Phyrexian, type {B/P}.

Uses Beleren Bold once it's loaded on the Name tab, and Cinzel until then. Try the pill or rounded box border for a P/T box.

A logo or icon works best. Use a PNG with a transparent background or an SVG. On a white background, the dark parts become the shape.

Symbols in braces become icons: {T} tap, {Q} untap, {W} {U} {B} {R} {G} {C}, numbers, {X}, {E} energy.
